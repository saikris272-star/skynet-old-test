About Us

Skynet Mobile Company is one of the leading companies in the field of technical for both men and women, with a wide selection of products ranging from mobiles and computers.

We at Skynet Mobiles are proud of our presence in the Kuwaiti market for more than nineteen years. The beginning was in 1996. When we open our first branch in farwaniya, Fire Station St. Thankfully, this branch drew in many customers, who were attracted by the variety of our products, our competitive prices and most importantly our keeping with all the new international fashion trends and styles. As the year went on and boosted by our customers support, Skynet Mobiles expanded. One branch at a time. Todat is has 4 branches, spread around the country to cover most of its areas with other branches opened for the sale of mobiles.

Skynet Mobile has staff of more than 24 employees of various nationalities, which provide ease in dealing with all the different nationalities in Kuwait. Our stated capital is $300,000.

Our auditor is AL Bassam & Co a member of Kreston International a worldwide network of independent accounts.

Our Aim

Our future aim is to expand our operations outside Kuwait by opening branches in the Middle East. Our targeted market for new are India and Dubai.
